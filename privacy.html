Privacy policy

Your privacy matters to us. This is a plain-language summary of how Fun Club handles information — not formal legal advice.

What we collect

The only personal information we collect is what you choose to share with us — for example, your name, email address, and the details you enter in our volunteer application form, or anything you include when emailing us. We use this information only to communicate with you and to support Fun Club's educational programs.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties.
  • This website does not use tracking cookies, advertising trackers, or analytics that identify you.
  • We do not build marketing profiles about visitors.

The volunteer form

Our volunteer application is a Google Form. Anything you submit through it is stored in Google's systems and is subject to Google's own privacy policy in addition to ours. Only Fun Club leadership reviews the responses, and we use them solely to consider you for a volunteer role.

Children and minors

Fun Club serves children, but we do not solicit information from minors directly through this website. Any information about a student or minor is only ever collected from a parent, guardian, or partnering school contact, and only for the purpose of running our programs safely.

Data requests

If you would like to know what information we have about you, correct it, or ask us to delete it, email funclub2020@gmail.com and we will do our best to respond promptly.

Disclaimer

This page is a plain-language summary intended to be easy to read. It is not formal legal advice. If you have specific legal questions about how your information is handled, please consult a qualified professional.
