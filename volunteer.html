Volunteer with Fun Club

Volunteers are the heart of Fun Club. Every role, big or small, helps us create a welcoming and encouraging learning environment for our students.

Roles

What volunteers do

Whether you're leading activities, helping students during lessons, creating educational materials, organizing events, or supporting behind the scenes, every role contributes to creating a welcoming and encouraging learning environment for our students.

Benefits

Why it matters (for you, too)

Volunteering with Fun Club is an opportunity to give back while developing valuable leadership, communication, teamwork, and organizational skills. Volunteers gain meaningful service experience, build lasting friendships, and become part of a community committed to making education more accessible and enjoyable for every child.

Expectations

What we ask of you

We ask our volunteers to be positive role models who arrive prepared, treat every student with kindness and respect, work collaboratively with other volunteers, and help create a safe, welcoming environment where every child feels encouraged to participate and learn.

Apply

Ready to join?

We welcome passionate students who are eager to learn, serve, and inspire others. Complete our volunteer application to tell us about yourself, your interests, and how you'd like to make a difference.
