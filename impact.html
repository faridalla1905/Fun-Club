By the numbers

Our impact so far

Since 2020, Fun Club has continued to grow through the dedication of student volunteers, families, and community members who believe in the power of education.

Beyond the numbers

Every lesson, workshop, and volunteer hour represents an opportunity to help children build confidence, strengthen their communication skills, and discover new interests. While numbers tell part of our story, our greatest impact can be seen in the students who become more confident learners and the volunteers who develop leadership through service.

We measure success in the small moments: a student who raises a hand for the first time, a volunteer who returns week after week, a family who feels welcomed. Those are the wins we celebrate.

Our story

Where Fun Club came from

Fun Club was created from our own experience as first-generation students adjusting to life in America. We know that learning English is only one part of the journey. Making friends, adapting to a new school system, and feeling like you belong can be just as challenging. We started Fun Club to be the mentors we once wished we had.

More than a tutoring program, Fun Club is a community where students build confidence, communication skills, friendships, and a love for learning through interactive workshops and creative activities. Our support doesn't stop when class ends — we're committed to helping students and their families navigate school, adjust to life in America, and grow both academically and socially.

We believe every child deserves the opportunity to thrive, and we're honored to be part of that journey.
