Our story

Fun Club began in 2020 with a simple idea: learning should be something children look forward to. Co-founded by Farid and Gulara Allahverdi, Fun Club started as a small student-led initiative dedicated to helping children strengthen their English skills through interactive lessons, games, and creative activities. Over the years, it has grown into a welcoming community where students, volunteers, and families come together to learn, build confidence, and support one another. Every lesson, workshop, and activity reflects our belief that education becomes more meaningful when it is engaging, encouraging, and accessible to everyone.

Mission

Our mission

To provide free, engaging, and inclusive educational opportunities that help children strengthen their English skills, build confidence, discover their potential, and develop a lifelong love of learning through creativity, collaboration, technology, and community service.

Vision

Our vision

To create a future where every child has access to meaningful educational experiences that inspire curiosity, confidence, leadership, and the belief that they can achieve their goals regardless of their background or learning style.

Meet the team

Our founders

Fun Club was co-founded in 2020 by siblings Farid and Gulara Allahverdi. Driven by a shared passion for education and community service, they created Fun Club to make learning English more engaging, interactive, and accessible for children. Together, they continue to develop programs, organize educational activities, and inspire student volunteers to make a positive impact in their community.
